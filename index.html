Project Overview

Construction plans are usually shared as 2D floor plans and elevation drawings, which can be hard for homeowners and subcontractors to picture. For our client, Buildertrend, we're building a pipeline that reads those drawings and automatically generates a 3D model of the building that you can rotate and explore in the browser.
